HEAT STRESS: 24-nt siRNA vs CHH METHYLATION
Final analysis summary
===========================================

DESIGN
------

Small-RNA dataset:
GSE239836
Arabidopsis thaliana Col-0
7-day seedlings
Control vs 1 h heat shock at 37 C
3 biological replicates per condition

Methylation dataset:
GSE139941
Arabidopsis thaliana Col-0
21-day leaves
Control 21 C vs 42 C heat for 6, 12, or 24 h
3 biological replicates per condition/time point

IMPORTANT:
These are independent cross-study datasets differing in tissue,
developmental age, temperature, and exposure duration.
Results therefore test cross-study spatial correspondence and
must not be interpreted as a matched-sample causal experiment.

CONCLUSION
----------

Heat stress is associated with substantial changes in both
24-nt small-RNA abundance and CHH methylation at the global
level in the analyzed public datasets.

However, heat-associated locus-specific changes in 24-nt
small-RNA abundance show essentially no spatial correspondence
with CHH methylation changes at 6, 12, or 24 h.

This result is robust to:
- 500-bp versus 100-bp resolution
- Pearson and Spearman correlation
- directional concordance
- strong delta24 tail analysis
- magnitude analysis
- adjustment for mean local sRNA and methylation activity

The absence of spatial correspondence must NOT be interpreted
as evidence that 24-nt siRNAs do not regulate CHH methylation
during heat stress, because the sRNA and methylation datasets
are from different experiments, tissues, developmental stages,
temperatures, and exposure durations.

Rather, the available cross-study heat datasets do not support
detectable genome-wide spatial coupling between the measured
heat-responsive 24-nt sRNA and CHH methylation profiles.
